World of MeMind LabLiving Earth

What can you notice that you usually miss?

Slow down long enough to discover how much information is already arriving through your body and the world around you.

Go explore

Spend ten silent minutes outside or by a window. Notice five things you can see, four you can hear, three you can feel, two you can smell, and one thing you suddenly became curious about.

Make something

Create a tiny “map of noticing” — drawing, voice note, collage, or list. Circle the thing you would have missed if you had been rushing.

Wonder

Does paying attention change what the world feels like?

World of UsMind LabTruth Quest

Can two people experience the same thing differently?

Explore perspective without deciding that one person has to be wrong.

Try it

Choose one picture, short story, object, or event. Koko, Israel, and the parent each describe what they notice first and what they think is happening — without hearing the others first.

Compare

What was the same? What was different? What did somebody else notice that you completely missed?

Wonder

When does a different perspective add information, and when do we need evidence to decide what is true?

03

Living EarthCreation StationMind Lab

What has nature already invented?

Nature has been solving problems for a very long time. Go looking for designs.

Find three designs

Look for something in nature that protects, carries, sticks, floats, cools, stores, hides, attracts, or moves efficiently.

Invent

Choose one and design a human object inspired by it. The goal is not to copy its appearance — copy the useful idea.

Wonder

What changes when humans see nature as a teacher instead of only as a resource?

04

Truth QuestTech WorldWorld of Me

Who is trying to get your attention?

Discover that attention is valuable — and many systems are designed to capture it.

Attention detective

Find five things designed to make someone look, click, watch, buy, or keep scrolling. They can be signs, packaging, videos, apps, games, or advertisements.

Ask

What feeling is each one trying to create? Who benefits if it works? Did you choose to pay attention, or did it pull you?

Create

Design an invitation that earns attention without tricking anyone.

Creation StationWorld of UsMake It Matter

What small problem could you make better?

Start with a person, not a product.

Notice a friction

Ask someone at home: “What is one tiny thing that makes your day harder or more annoying?” Listen without immediately solving it.

Prototype

Build the fastest possible version of an improvement using paper, cardboard, blocks, drawing, or simple code.

Test

Give it back to the person. Ask what helped, what did not, and what they would change.

Tech WorldAI AdventureTruth Quest

When should a machine help — and when should a human decide?

Technology can be powerful without being the right decision-maker for every situation.

Sort the decisions

Make three columns: MACHINE CAN HELP, HUMAN SHOULD DECIDE, and WE NEED BOTH. Sort examples like choosing a song, diagnosing a broken toy, deciding who gets punished, translating a language, suggesting a route, or choosing a friend.

Go deeper

Ask what information a machine would need, what it might miss, and who is responsible if the result hurts someone.

Continue

Enter AI Adventure when you are ready to explore machine intelligence more deeply.

The UnknownMind LabWorld of Us

What do we do with questions that do not have answers yet?

Some questions are problems to solve. Others are places to live in for a while.

Build a Question Constellation

Everyone contributes three questions they genuinely do not know the answer to. No searching yet. Group questions that seem connected.

Choose one

Decide whether it is factual, philosophical, personal, scientific, imaginative, or something else entirely.

Wonder

Can not knowing be useful?

ALL WORLDS

What will you do with what you have noticed?

The final adventure is not a test. It is a contribution.

Choose something you care about

A person, animal, place, problem, idea, feeling, or question.

Create a response

Make something that helps, teaches, protects, invites, explains, improves, delights, or makes somebody wonder. Use any medium you want.

Tell the story

“I noticed… I wondered… I explored… I changed my mind about… I created… and now I want to…”

There is no finish line for curiosity

Every answer can become another doorway.

Return to any world. Repeat an adventure differently. Follow a question that was not in the plan.
