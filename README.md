# WOW — World of Wonder

A conscious learning world for curious kids exploring self, mind, nature, relationships, creativity, technology, discernment, and what it means to be human.

> **The world is full of things worth wondering about.**

WOW is designed first for children and the adults learning alongside them. It is not a conventional school portal or a collection of worksheets. Children enter worlds, follow questions, notice what is around and within them, experiment, make things, reflect, and contribute.

## Start here: two layers

WOW now has **two connected learning experiences**:

1. **The 8-Adventure Journey** — the playful entry point. Eight cross-world missions designed to help children feel how WOW works.
2. **The 24-Week Foundation Curriculum** — the full ages 7–9 program. Six four-week learning arcs with explicit big ideas, experiences, foundation/stretch paths, parent lenses, reflection, creation, and contribution.

The Journey is not the curriculum. It is the doorway into it.

## The 24-week foundation

### Arc 01 · Learning to Notice — Weeks 1–4
Self-awareness, senses, attention, emotion, perspective, and patterns.

### Arc 02 · Living With Others — Weeks 5–8
Empathy, boundaries, communication, consent, conflict, cooperation, and repair.

### Arc 03 · Learning From a Living World — Weeks 9–12
Ecology, systems, interdependence, biomimicry, resources, stewardship, and place.

### Arc 04 · Truth, Attention & Influence — Weeks 13–16
Evidence, persuasion, advertising, incentives, media literacy, uncertainty, and trust.

### Arc 05 · Humans, Tools & Intelligent Machines — Weeks 17–20
Engineering, computation, privacy, cybersecurity, algorithms, AI, agency, and human judgment.

### Arc 06 · Imagination, Purpose & Contribution — Weeks 21–24
Creativity, entrepreneurship, mystery, values, problem-solving, service, and meaningful creation.

Each week is designed for roughly **45–75 minutes** and can expand into multiple sessions if a question catches fire.

## Core learning rhythm

**NOTICE → WONDER → EXPLORE → QUESTION → CREATE → REFLECT → CONTRIBUTE**

## The worlds

1. **World of Me** — self-awareness, emotions, body, senses, attention, intuition, identity.
2. **Mind Lab** — curiosity, imagination, memory, logic, metacognition, critical thinking.
3. **Living Earth** — ecology, animals, plants, systems, interdependence, stewardship.
4. **World of Us** — empathy, communication, boundaries, cooperation, consent, community.
5. **Creation Station** — art, storytelling, invention, design, making, entrepreneurship.
6. **Truth Quest** — evidence, discernment, media literacy, persuasion, misinformation, incentives.
7. **Tech World** — computers, coding, internet literacy, privacy, cybersecurity, engineering.
8. **AI Adventure** — the existing standalone AI-learning journey, connected as one world within WOW.
9. **The Unknown** — philosophy, consciousness, space, mysteries, unanswered questions, wonder.
10. **Make It Matter** — purpose, service, contribution, ethics, meaningful problem-solving.

## Design principles

- The **child is the center**. Technology is one part of the world they learn to navigate.
- Start with lived experience before abstraction.
- Questions count as learning.
- Creation matters more than completion.
- Different ages can share one experience with different depth.
- The adult is a guide and co-learner, not an all-knowing authority.
- Avoid overstimulation: calm layouts, optional animation, dark/light themes, gentle motion, clear hierarchy.
- No public child profiles, private learning records, surnames, schools, locations, or identifying information.

## Relationship to AI Adventure

AI Adventure remains a separate project because AI literacy changes quickly and deserves its own complete pathway. WOW connects to it as the **AI Adventure** world while keeping the larger curriculum centered on whole-human development.
