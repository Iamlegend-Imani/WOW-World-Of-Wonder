For the guide learning too

You do not have to know everything.

WOW works best when the adult models curiosity, uncertainty, discernment, and the willingness to change their mind.

The first WOW journey

Move across worlds, not through school subjects.

The eight adventures intentionally combine self-awareness, nature, relationships, critical thinking, technology, creativity, and contribution. Follow the question wherever it leads instead of stopping because it crossed into another “subject.”

Open the 8-adventure journey

The role

Guide the discovery. Do not own it.

Ask before explaining

Invite a prediction, theory, feeling, or observation before giving information.

Let mistakes stay visible

Do not rush to fix every wrong answer. Ask what the mistake teaches.

Model uncertainty

Say, “I don’t know. How could we find out?” without embarrassment.

Protect agency

Help the children use tools without surrendering judgment, privacy, imagination, or choice.

One experience, two depths

Koko and Israel stay together.

Foundation path

Observe, sort, draw, name, compare, tell a story, explain in your own words.

Stretch path

Predict, test, compare evidence, find exceptions, explain causes, challenge assumptions.

Neither path is a rank. A child may need different depth depending on the topic, energy, or day.

The WOW rhythm

Seven moves. No grades required.

  1. NOTICEWhat do you observe?
  2. WONDERWhat are you curious about?
  3. EXPLOREInvestigate it.
  4. QUESTIONWhat deserves a closer look?
  5. CREATEMake something from what you learned.
  6. REFLECTWhat changed in your understanding?
  7. CONTRIBUTEHow could this matter beyond you?
